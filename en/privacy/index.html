Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29

Liftwise is made by Evgeniy Shvets, an independent developer. This policy describes what happens to your data in the Liftwise app for iPhone and Apple Watch, its widgets and Live Activity. Short version: we do not collect any data.

Your data stays on your device

Everything you enter or record in Liftwise stays on your devices: your profile and onboarding answers, programs, workouts and sets, measurements and progress photos, food diary and nutrition plans, and app settings.

Liftwise has no server and no user account. The app does not send your data to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to see it.

Your iPhone and Apple Watch exchange workout data directly with each other through Apple's system connection. Widgets and the Live Activity read data from shared storage on the same device.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Liftwise reads from Apple Health: workouts, active energy, heart rate, body weight and height. It uses them only on your device to show your activity and progress and to fill in your weight and height during setup.

If you allow it, Liftwise writes the workouts you finish in the app to Apple Health.

Data from Apple Health never leaves your device through Liftwise. It is not used for advertising or marketing, is not shared with third parties and is not stored in iCloud by Liftwise. You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Liftwise.

Backup file

In More → Your data → Export backup you can create a backup file. It contains all of your Liftwise data, including progress photos, and is not encrypted. You decide where to save or send it (for example, Files or iCloud Drive); once it leaves the app, it is handled by the service you chose. Keep it somewhere safe.

Sharing a program

When you share a program, Liftwise creates a .liftwise file with the program only: its name, goal, place, level, days, exercises with their planned sets, reps and weights, and your exercise notes. Your workout history, logged results, one-rep maximums, measurements and profile are never included. The file is sent through the share sheet with the app you choose.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. Liftwise only receives from Apple the status of your subscription to unlock Liftwise Pro; we never see your payment details, name or Apple Account.

The start date of your free trial week is stored in your device's Keychain so that deleting data does not restart it.

No analytics, ads or tracking

Liftwise contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites. The only crash information we may see is the anonymous reports Apple provides to developers if you have chosen to share them in your device settings.

Notifications

Rest timer alerts are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone or Apple Watch. Nothing is sent through a server.

Deleting your data

You can delete all of your data in More → Your data → Delete my data, or by deleting the app. Data you have written to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app.

Children

Liftwise does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If a future version of Liftwise starts processing data off your device, the policy will be updated before that version is released.
