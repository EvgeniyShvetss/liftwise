Support

Last updated: 2026-10-29

Need help with Liftwise, found a bug or have an idea? Write to liftwise.app@gmail.com. We answer in English, Russian and Ukrainian, usually within 2 business days.

Please include

  • your iPhone and Apple Watch models and iOS/watchOS versions;
  • the app version (Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Liftwise, or the App Store page);
  • what you did, what you expected and what happened, ideally with a screenshot.

Frequently asked questions

How does the free week work? Liftwise Pro is free for 7 days from the first launch. Nothing is charged afterwards unless you buy a monthly or yearly subscription yourself.

How do I restore my subscription? Open More → Liftwise Pro → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel a subscription? iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Liftwise Pro.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone? More → Your data → Export backup creates a backup file; open it on the new iPhone in Liftwise (More → Your data → Import copy). An iPhone backup or Quick Start transfer also moves the app's data.

Workouts don't appear in Apple Health. Check access in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Liftwise.

How do I share a program? Open the program and tap Share: the recipient gets a .liftwise file and opens it in Liftwise.

How do I delete my data? More → Your data → Delete my data. See the Privacy Policy.
